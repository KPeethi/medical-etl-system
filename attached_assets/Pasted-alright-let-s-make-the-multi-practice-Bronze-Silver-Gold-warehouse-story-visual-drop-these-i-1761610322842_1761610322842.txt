### Usage notes (keep it crisp)

* **Bronze is sacred**: everything there is copy-only. Router never mutates source.
* **Plan → Execute**: you always dry-run first; if unmapped% > 60, it blocks (409).
* **Roster Autodetect**: preview with `/api/identity/preview` before real runs.
* **Per-practice config** comes from the Practice Pack (paths, roster, keywords).
* **Logs feed warehouse**: CSV mirrors → `fact_fileprocessing`, `dim_*`, `audit_*`.
* **Dash** (or Postman) pulls `/api/stats`, `/api/unmapped`, `/api/recent-runs`.
